One instrument, one traceable path from registration to certificate

Every test suite moves through the same four stages, and no single person can push it through alone.

1

Register the instrument

Capacity, scale interval, verification interval and accuracy class are checked against the legal n-range before anything can be tested.

2

Run the test suite

Accuracy, eccentricity, repeatability and environmental checks are evaluated against the applicable MPE at each load.

3

Submit for review

The report is locked to the tester who ran it — identity comes from the signed session, never a typed-in name.

4

Approve & certify

An approver signs off or rejects with a reason. Approved reports generate the final certificate PDF.

Separation of duties, enforced by the system

Testing and approval are different roles on different accounts — so the person who produced a number is never the person who signs off on it.

Tester

  • Registers instruments and their metrological parameters
  • Submits accuracy, eccentricity and repeatability test suites
  • Creates a revision after a rejection
  • Cannot approve or reject any report — including their own

Approver

  • Reviews submitted reports against the recorded readings
  • Approves or rejects, with a reason on rejection
  • Cannot register instruments or submit test data
  • Can never become the source of the numbers they're checking

Accounts are provisioned by an administrator; there's no self-serve sign-up. Log in with the credentials you were issued.
